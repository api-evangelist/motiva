# Motiva AI

> Motiva AI is an artificial-intelligence layer for enterprise email marketing that plugs into Oracle Eloqua and other marketing-automation platforms. It automates campaign decision-making with multivariate (A/B/n) message testing, patented send-time optimization, frequency management, list/dark-pool cleanup, audience discovery, and generative-AI email content. It is delivered as an installed connector rather than a public developer API.

## Products
- [Motiva Message Testing AI](https://www.motiva.ai/products/): A/B/n and multivariate email message testing across multi-step campaigns
- [Motiva Send Time AI](https://www.motiva.ai/products/): U.S.-patented per-contact send-time optimization
- [Motiva Frequency Management](https://www.motiva.ai/products/): prevents over-emailing of contacts
- [Motiva Smart Suppress](https://www.motiva.ai/products/): removes problematic contacts to clean a segment
- [Motiva Generator](https://www.motiva.ai/products-motiva-generator/): generative-AI email content, including private-label custom LLMs
